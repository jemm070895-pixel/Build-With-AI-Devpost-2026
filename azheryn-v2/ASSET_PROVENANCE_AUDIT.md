## Decision
Do not mark visual rights/originality audit complete. This audit is not evidence that any identified asset infringes rights; it documents that provenance has not yet been independently established. Do not claim 100% original or fully cleared media until the evidence above is available.

## Owner testimony — 2026-10-24
The owner explicitly states: (1) the Fehe is entirely their original creative design; ChatGPT generated its visual representation from the owner's description; (2) the other game illustrations were generated by ChatGPT, and the owner does not know what external sources, if any, were used. This is first-party testimony about the creative process, not independent proof of exclusive copyright or a completed third-party input audit.

AI-generated images are not automatically copied from a specific external image, and the absence of a known external source does not establish that all inputs or outputs are rights-cleared. Record original prompts, generation records and any supplied reference images when available. Avoid claims of guaranteed exclusive copyright over wholly AI-generated pixels; human-authored creative elements and applicable terms/law must be assessed separately.
