# Devpost submission draft — AZHERYN V2: Chapter 1

## Inspiration
What if founding a civilization began not with a finished city, but with a clearing, a handful of people and the decision to build together? AZHERYN V2 starts at that first step.

## What it does
AZHERYN V2 is a bilingual browser strategy game. The player guides Mui and seven former guards through a wooded valley. They select trees, collect timber with two-person hauling teams, organize a stockpile and construct the first functional warehouse. Chapter 1 concludes after the 91-log storehouse is completed. A final screen previews the much larger vision: food, settlements, markets, kingdoms and evolving societies. Those later systems are planned, not implemented in this submission.

## How we built it
The Chapter 1 prototype uses React, TypeScript and Vite, with custom procedural world logic and gameplay systems. Development was iterative, with repeated audits, TypeScript corrections and local Windows playtests. The public repository includes the Devpost Learn Skill Pack and planning artifacts (scope, PRD and technical specification). A separate MIT-licensed utilities folder demonstrates reusable procedural code.

## Challenges
Maintaining reliable two-person log transport, stable NPC task assignment, readable mobile-friendly UI and a natural-looking forest while keeping the contest chapter small enough to finish. A late TypeScript tuple mismatch in G6.31 was corrected for G6.31.1.

## Accomplishments
A complete Chapter 1 flow focused on cooperative labor and the first building, Spanish/English presentation, story introduction, mission journal and a forward-looking epilogue. The owner confirmed the G6.31.1 game works on their Windows laptop on 2026-10-23.

## What is next
Expand beyond the clearing: food systems, villages, trade and markets, social dynamics, kingdoms and eventually the Academy. The contest submission represents the beginning of the larger project, not its full scope.

React · TypeScript · Vite · JavaScript · HTML · CSS · GitHub · AI-assisted development
