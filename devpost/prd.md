# AZHERYN V2 — Product Requirements

## Product statement
AZHERYN V2 is a strategy game in which the player influences Mui and a small founding group as they transform a natural environment into the beginning of a settlement and, over the long term, the society that can give rise to the Academy.

## Devpost Chapter 1 journey
1. The game opens on the wooded valley and central clearing.
2. Mui and seven former guards are present in/near the clearing.
3. The player can inspect the environment at useful strategic scale.
4. The group establishes itself and identifies trees that can be cut.
5. Tree cutting produces wood and physically frees space.
6. The player chooses where the first functional structure should go.
7. Characters perform the required work rather than the building appearing without cause.
8. The first warehouse/storage building becomes functional.
9. The result visibly proves the founding loop and points toward later settlement and Academy development.

## Player role
The player is the strategic decision-maker/owner perspective associated with Mui, but Mui remains a character rather than a simple cursor. The longer-term design may use a mental/intuitive player↔Mui relationship. That deeper system is not required for Chapter 1.

## People
- Mui plus seven male former royal guards.
- Guards begin with defense experience and can learn other activities.
- Each receives an exceptional short-bladed object from Mui; its final name, material and geometry remain unresolved.
- Character detail should eventually be recognizable at close camera scale.
- No unresolved mineral name or Saga fact is invented for the contest.

## World requirements
The starting region is a broad wooded valley with:
- meaningful numeric relief;
- a permanent river;
- variable river width/depth;
- shallow or narrow crossings;
- irregular riverbanks/wet areas where appropriate;
- a roughly central irregular clearing;
- dense and sparse forest structure;
- natural boundaries rather than obvious invisible walls.

The world should avoid obvious grids, mosaics and artificial geometric patches.

## Resource and construction requirements
Wood is the first managed resource.

Cutting a tree must:
- remove/alter that tree in the persistent world;
- produce usable wood;
- free physical terrain.

The first construction target is a functional warehouse/storage building. Its placement, material use and completion must be understandable to the player.

## Camera and readability
The strategic camera must support movement and zoom. Later META 1.3 work should allow sufficiently close inspection to recognize characters, clothing, conversations, knives, wood and tools. 180-degree rotation is postponed.

## Visual requirements
G1 is the development baseline:
- rustic and attractive;
- readable isometric/top-down strategy presentation;
- natural terrain and water;
- irregular vegetation;
- recognizable people/resources/buildings when relevant;
- coherent scale;
- acceptable performance.

After the end-to-end Chapter 1 loop works, perform a contest polish phase toward G2 if time permits.

## Demo success criteria
In roughly 1–3 minutes, a judge should be able to understand:
- who Mui and the founding group are;
- where they are;
- that the environment is a physical strategic space;
- that cutting trees creates both a resource and settlement space;
- that construction follows player decisions and character work;
- that the warehouse is the first step of a much larger historical strategy game.

## Non-goals for Chapter 1
Long-term systems and narrative content outside the contest slice remain private.

## Historical evidence
Earlier prototype history is retained privately.



## Private roadmap
Long-term simulation, discovery, knowledge and world-system architecture is maintained in the private development repository and is outside the public contest slice.
