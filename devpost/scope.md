# AZHERYN V2 — Scope

## Core idea
AZHERYN V2 is a strategy game about founding and developing a society that can eventually become the Academy. The Devpost contest slice deliberately begins earlier: with Mui and seven former guards arriving in a wooded valley and creating the first functional settlement infrastructure.

## Contest proof of concept — Chapter 1
Playable chain:

**Mui + 7 former guards → establish in/near the central clearing → cut trees → obtain wood and free terrain → choose a building location → construct → complete the first functional warehouse/storage building.**

Success means this chain works end-to-end, is understandable in a short demo, and looks attractive enough to communicate the larger game.

## Starting group
- Mui.
- Seven male NPCs, former guards of the King.
- Eight people total.
- Guards initially know defense and can learn other work.
- Each guard has a rustic knife gifted by Mui.
- The knife mineral remains unnamed and must not be canonized here.

## Initial managed resource
Wood is the first managed resource. Cutting trees both produces wood and opens terrain for settlement.

Water, food, stone and other materials may exist physically but are not automatically part of the initial managed economy.

## Initial world
A broad wooded valley with a permanent river and an irregular, roughly central clearing almost surrounded by forest.

The river varies in width and depth and includes shallow/narrow crossings. Terrain, forest and clearing should look natural rather than geometric.

## Development metas
- META 1 — Mundo y geografía: ACTIVE.
- META 1.1 — Terreno base: APPROVED/CLOSED.
- META 1.2 — Río y agua geográfica: NEXT.
- META 1.3 — Cámara y visualización.
- META 1.4 — Apariencia natural del terreno.
- META 1.5 — Bosque estructural.
- META 1.6 — Integración y prueba.
- Flora and Fauna remain later separate metas.

## Visual target
Development target G1: readable, attractive, rustic/isometric strategy presentation with coherent scale and acceptable performance. After Chapter 1 works end-to-end, polish toward G2 or better if time permits.

## Explicit exclusions from the contest slice
- The Academy does not need to be built in Chapter 1.
- No global ranking is required.
- No 15/16-Academy system is required.
- No complete historical progression is required.
- No full economy is required.
- No photorealistic/open-world 3D target.
- No forced canonization of unresolved Saga details.

## Future
The Academy, knowledge systems, broader historical progression, other Academies, competition and legacy/cycle systems belong to later development. The project continues after the hackathon.

## Historical note
The previous Academy-first scope is preserved under `devpost/v1-history/` and in Git history. It remains evidence of the learning/build process but is not the current contest target.
